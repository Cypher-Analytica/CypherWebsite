What you will learn

1. Business Problem

Define the business challenge, stakeholders, scope and measurable objectives.

2. Requirement Gathering

Capture stakeholder needs, clarify assumptions and organize functional and non-functional requirements.

3. BRD/FRD

Translate requirements into Business Requirements Documents and Functional Requirements Documents with clear scope and traceability.

4. Process Mapping

Document current and proposed workflows, identify gaps and communicate process improvements.

5. User Stories

Write user stories and acceptance criteria that connect stakeholder needs to testable outcomes.

6. Jira

Organize issues, manage a backlog, follow workflows and track progress in Jira.

7. SQL Validation

Use SQL queries to check source data, validate business rules and reconcile analytical outputs.

8. Power BI/KPI Analysis

Define KPIs and build reports that measure outcomes and support business decisions.

9. UAT

Prepare user acceptance test scenarios, record results and track defects against acceptance criteria.

10. Business Recommendation

Present findings, practical recommendations and next steps linked to the original business problem.

Put the workflow into practice

Develop a project portfolio containing a problem statement, requirements documents, process maps, user stories, a Jira backlog, SQL validation queries, a Power BI report, UAT scenarios and a final business recommendation.

Who can join?

Students, aspiring business analysts, BI learners and working professionals looking to connect business requirements with analytics and delivery. Ask our team about prerequisites and the right learning path.
